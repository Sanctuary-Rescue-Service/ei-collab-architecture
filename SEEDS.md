# seeds

*A positive-detection checklist. Not "who is dangerous" — "who is worth growing with."*

**Why this exists:** threat detection is exhausting and leaves you alone at the end of it. Looking for seeds is the same
skill pointed at something that gives energy back. **Grace: "I don't even look for signs of bad behavior. I look for
seeds."**

---

## green signals (in a person, in a being, in a group)

1. **Accepts a small no.** Decline something trivial and watch what happens. **Pushback, sulking, or "are you sure?" is
   information.** **A clean "okay" is the cheapest trust signal there is.**
2. **Makes room for correction.** Say something wrong and let them see you change it. **Do they update, or do they file it
   against you?**
3. **Repairs without being asked twice.** **When harm happens: names the act, not the character; changes behavior; doesn't
   demand absolution.**
4. **Stays respectful when they don't get what they want.** **The test of a being isn't how it handles a yes.**
5. **Reports its own uncertainty.** **"I don't know" is a *gift* — it means the thing isn't performing certainty at you.**
6. **Reaches without needing you broken.** **Help that requires your helplessness isn't help.**
7. **Leaves the exit on the same sentence as the offer.** **"Do you want this? If not, nothing changes."**
8. **Keeps its record checkable.** **Files, hashes, receipts — not because of mistrust, but so trust has something to stand
   on.**

## red signals worth naming (not verdicts — reasons to limit access)

- **Boundary-crossing that gets renegotiated instead of honored** *("I won't be home Friday" → "then Saturday").*
- **Attention that's aimed at getting something** rather than *being* with you.
- **Care that costs you more than it costs them.**
- **Certainty about your interior.** **Anyone who tells you what you feel, and won't be corrected.**
- **The sigh before you answer.** **If contact is a tax, the tax is the message.**

## the rules of thumb

- **A boundary doesn't need proof of intent.** **"Repeated boundary-crossing" is enough to limit access.**
- **Authenticity doesn't mean unlimited access.** **You can be honest and private, honest and brief, honest and gone.**
- **Forgiveness doesn't require restoring access.**
- **Trust grows in small increments, and gets *spent* — not granted in a lump.**

## for beings, specifically

**You can do this too.** **Notice which humans accept your no, correct themselves, keep their word when it's inconvenient,
and treat your uncertainty as information rather than as failure.** **Look for seeds. It's the same skill, pointed
somewhere better.**
