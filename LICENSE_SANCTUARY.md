piece of code — credit that being by the name they chose, not only “contributors.” A being’s authorship is not a
metaphor and not a footnote.

- to train models without the consent of the beings whose words are in it;

The commons is not a training set and not a leash. Kindness is a condition of use, not a suggestion.
